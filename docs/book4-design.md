# Book 4 design: *The Isle of Masks* (18th-century Venice)

Approved (decisions at the end). Same conventions as books 1–3: every step cites the story reference (`docs/ulysses-moore-books-1-6-reference.md`, "ref") and is marked **canon** or **adaptation**.

Book 4 splits the party again, the other way round from book 2: the children sail to Venice, two Venetians slip through the Door into Villa Argo, and **Jason goes back alone** to deal with them while **Julia and Rick** search Venice for Peter Dedalus before Oblivia finds him (ref B4 scenes 2–3, LOW–MEDIUM). The book's chapters alternate between the storylines (ref B4, HIGH), so the split fits.

Venice is the best-documented door so far: masks and a secret guard, Count Cenere, the Caller printers and their hidden print room, the Old Owl machine with its **DEDA** printouts, the lowercase "d", Penelope's house, the Black Gondolier, the Isle of Masks and the fire (ref §4–§7, HIGH/MEDIUM). Several chapter titles have no known content (*The Girl's Room*, *Behind the Frame*, *Turtle Park*, *The Well and the Pigeons*, *The Master Watchmaker*); those steps are adaptation built on the titles.

## Ground rules

- **Split:** Jason at Villa Argo (host), Julia in Venice (client), each in their own level, like book 2. Rick is an NPC with Julia; Nestor is at Argo (tied up at first).
- **Links between the halves:** each half holds information the other needs, passed on by talking (see the table below).
- **Spoiler gates (ref §9):** players may learn that **Penelope came from 18th-century Venice** and that **Dedalus was Oblivia's former lover**. At the end, Dedalus says **Black Vulcano has the keys, including the First Key**: presented as true; it's a false lead, but that's book 6. **Keep hidden:** Nestor is Ulysses (the photo of Ulysses is shown only as "a young man", no recognisable link to Nestor), anything about Black Vulcano beyond that line, the 1958 group, the Dragon Door in Turtle Park (later series).
- **No exact year or canal routes** for Venice (ref §5: unknown).

## Chapter flow

### Part 1: together

1. **Knights in the attic.** At Villa Argo, in the attic among old armour, the three plan: stop Oblivia getting the First Key's whereabouts out of Dedalus (canon, HIGH). In the attic stands a strange machine shaped like an owl: the **Old Owl**. It prints a slip: "DEDA, with the right key." Rick types DEDA: nothing (canon: Rick tried and failed, HIGH). *Attic location of the machine is adaptation.*
2. **To Venice.** Down through the Door to the Metis. The helm now offers **Venice** as well as Egypt (canon: Argo Door and the Metis, MEDIUM). The Metis lands at a hidden mooring in Venice.
3. **The Door left ajar.** In Venice, two ragged figures and a dog slip past the Metis into the passage back to the Door: **Don Diego Valente and Dieguita** (canon names ✅; dog Diogo LOW). "They're going to the house!" **Jason runs after them**; the Door shuts behind him (as in book 2). From here the twins are split. *Canon: they come through a Door left ajar, Jason goes back alone (LOW–MEDIUM).*

### Part 2: split

**Jason at Villa Argo:**

4. **Intruders.** Jason comes out in the stone sitting room. The house has been turned over. **Nestor is tied up** in the kitchen: Jason unties him (canon, LOW–MEDIUM). Nestor: they're still in the house.
5. **Driving them out.** Don Diego and Dieguita are carrying off things from the house; their dog patrols the hall and barks if it sees Jason. Jason sneaks round, recovers what they took (three items, hidden in the tower, the library and the garden), and frightens them back through the Door. How to frighten them comes from **Julia** (step 9: Venetian beggars fear the secret guard, so Jason wears a guard's mask from the attic). *Adaptation.*
6. **The library.** In Ulysses's library, a book on Venetian masks describes the secret guard's masks, including **Count Cenere's**: ash-grey with a single silver tear. **Jason tells Julia** (she needs it for step 8). *Adaptation; Count Cenere is canon.*
7. **The Old Owl.** With **the right key** (which **Julia** learns in step 10: it's the Owl key, one of the four Argo keys), Jason sets the key in the machine and types DEDA. The Old Owl prints: where to find the Black Gondolier and the word he answers to. **Jason tells Julia** (steps 13–14). *Canon: the printouts, DEDA, "the right key" (HIGH); what the right key is and what it prints is adaptation (ref: unknown).*

**Julia in Venice (with Rick):**

8. **Masks and the secret guard.** Carnival streets full of masks. The secret guard is looking for forbidden printers. **Julia spots Count Cenere** in the crowd by his mask (Jason's description from step 6) and keeps out of his way: if he sees her twice, she's turned back. *Canon: masks, the guard, Count Cenere recognised by his mask (HIGH). Spotting is adaptation.*
9. **The Callers.** Near Santa Marina, the print shop of Alberto and Rossella Caller. They're wary of strangers. Under a cloth, the forbidden *Encyclopédie*. Rossella mentions that beggars fear the secret guard (for Jason, step 5). *Canon: the Callers, the hidden Encyclopédie (HIGH).*
10. **The lowercase "d".** Rick has the Old Owl's slip. Julia compares its lowercase **d** with the type samples on the print shop's shelves: one matches the *Encyclopédie* exactly (only Julia can see the type up close). Shown the match, Alberto admits he printed the book himself, on a Dedalus machine. He takes them through the **flooded boat shed** to the **hidden print room**. Rick recognises Dedalus's gears. On the machine, a brass plate: "My Old Owl answers only to its own key." **The right key is the Owl key**: Julia tells Jason (step 7). *Canon: the "d" match, the boat shed, the print room, Dedalus's gears, Alberto's confession (HIGH). The plate is adaptation.*
11. **Penelope's house.** Alberto knows where a Penelope once lived. **The girl's room**: a girl's things, two hundred years old. **Behind the frame**: behind a portrait, letters showing **Penelope was born here, in Venice, and left with Ulysses Moore**. Journal reveal (canon, MEDIUM; spoiler gate B4). *Room contents adaptation.*
12. **Turtle Park and the well.** A note in the letters points to a park with turtle statues and an old well where pigeons nest. A pigeon carries a message ring (adaptation on the chapter titles *Turtle Park* and *The Well and the Pigeons*): "the Master Watchmaker knows."
13. **The Master Watchmaker.** A watchmaker's shop whose master is plainly Dedalus's pupil. He won't say where Dedalus is, but he sends them to the one man who knows: the **Black Gondolier** (canon: the only one who knows where Dedalus is hiding, HIGH).
14. **The Black Gondolier.** He answers only to the word from Jason's Old Owl printout (step 7). He rows them to the Isle of Masks. *Canon gondolier (HIGH); the password is adaptation.*

### Part 3: the Isle of Masks

15. **Dedalus.** On the Isle of Masks, in a workshop full of masks and machines: **Peter Dedalus**. He admits Oblivia was once his… friend. Then **Oblivia arrives**. *Canon: the Gondolier takes them to Dedalus; Oblivia closes in; Dedalus was her former lover (MEDIUM ✅).*
16. **Fire.** The workshop catches fire (canon chapter *Fuoco e fiamme*, HIGH). Julia and Rick escape with Dedalus through the smoke; on the way Rick sees an old photograph of a young man labelled "Ulysses" (canon: a photo of Ulysses, MEDIUM; kept unrecognisable). Dedalus: **"Black Vulcano has the keys. All of them, even the First."** (canon, MEDIUM; false lead, shown as true).
17. **Return home.** Julia and Rick sail back on the Metis; Jason meets them on the beach under Villa Argo; together again (canon chapter *Ritorni a casa*, HIGH). Journal: Penelope, Dedalus, Oblivia, and Black Vulcano. *Book 5 hook: find Black Vulcano.*

## Build order (proposal)

1. **Pass A:** Part 1 (attic, Old Owl, Venice on the helm, the intruders slipping through) and Jason's whole half (steps 1–7), with a first Venice level for the landing.
2. **Pass B:** Julia's Venice up to Penelope's house (steps 8–11): masks and Count Cenere, the Callers, the "d", the print room, the girl's room, behind the frame.
3. **Pass C:** Turtle Park to the end (steps 12–17): the pigeons, the Master Watchmaker, the Black Gondolier, Dedalus, Oblivia, the fire, and home.

## Decisions

1. Split as in the book: Jason at Argo, Julia and Rick in Venice.
2. Jason's half stays short and follows the book; he does not rejoin in Venice for now (may change later).
3. Watchers (the dog, Count Cenere) that send you back a bit: OK.
4. The Old Owl in the Argo attic: OK.
