# Book 3 design: *The House of Mirrors* (Kilmore Cove)

Approved (decisions at the end). Same conventions as books 1 and 2: every step cites the story reference (`docs/ulysses-moore-books-1-6-reference.md`, "ref") and is marked **canon** or **adaptation**.

Book 3 is different from 1 and 2: **nobody goes through a Door** (ref B3, MEDIUM). It's an investigation at home. Kilmore Cove itself is the puzzle: missing from maps, no phone signal, a railway that leads nowhere, a statue of a king who never reigned (ref §3, HIGH: "plot mysteries, not mistakes to fix"). It ends at Peter Dedalus's **House of Mirrors**, where Oblivia slips through a Door to Venice.

The reference has the plot and the house's contents (illusions, machines, a chess game across the centuries, an owl clock, a black Bakelite telephone) but **no solutions** (§8: "needs invention"). So the puzzles here are mostly adaptation, built from those canon objects.

## Ground rules

- **Together again:** the twins are not split in book 3. The asymmetry comes back to **private information**: in the House of Mirrors, each twin literally sees a different house.
- **Rick** stays an NPC who follows the twins and gives hints.
- **Spoiler gates (ref §9):** players may learn that the town itself is a mystery, that Dedalus built strange machines, that **there are other Doors**, and that **one key opens them all** (the First Key). The Venice trail appears only near the end. **Keep hidden:** Nestor is Ulysses (he can be "connected to Ulysses", no more); Black Vulcano and the station (book 5); the 1958 Great Summer (book 5); Penelope came from Venice (book 4); Rick's father (book 6). The station can be seen but stays locked.
- **Language** (ref §3 rule 6, HIGH): Jason could understand Egyptian in Punt but can't now.

## Chapter flow

### Part 1: home (morning)

1. **Breakfast.** Everyone at Villa Argo after book 2. Nestor serves breakfast; the twins' journals now share both halves of book 2 (each twin can read the other's Punt and storm entries from here on). *Canon: reunion breakfast, comparing stories (HIGH). Shared journal is adaptation.*
2. **Lost in translation.** Jason looks at the Egyptian writing on the papyrus drawing he copied in Punt: it's gibberish now. Journal entry. *Canon (HIGH; the explanation is unknown, so the game doesn't give one).*

### Part 2: the impossible village

3. **Four impossibilities.** Ride down to Kilmore Cove. The journal opens a checklist, "Things that are wrong with Kilmore Cove", ticked off as the twins find them. *Canon list (HIGH); checklist is adaptation.*
   - **No maps:** in **Calypso's bookshop**, **Julia** checks the atlases: Kilmore Cove isn't in any of them.
   - **No signal:** the phone box in the square gets only a hum.
   - **Tracks to nowhere:** **Jason** follows the old railway line to where it vanishes into a sealed tunnel (the station stays locked: book 5).
   - **The king who never reigned:** the statue in the square. **Jason** climbs the plinth to read the worn inscription; **Julia** looks the name up in the bookshop's history of English kings: there was no such king.
4. **The old tourist guide.** With the list complete, **Calypso** gives the twins an old tourist guide to Kilmore Cove, the kind no one has printed in decades (canon, HIGH). Only **Julia** reads it (it's in tiny print; she's the reader). It lists landmarks: the House of Mirrors, the lighthouse, a house "famous for its cats", and more.
5. **What was on the map.** Jason held the map for ten seconds in Punt (book 2). He remembers **marks** on it, shown only in his journal: a little drawing at three places, but not which places. **Julia's guide** names the landmarks; together they work out which landmarks had marks. One of them is the House of Mirrors. *Canon: the guide plus what they remember of the map proves the town's hidden history (HIGH). The marks-to-landmarks puzzle is adaptation.*
6. **The old residents.** Talking around town: **Calypso**, **Leonard Minaxo** at the lighthouse, and **Miss Biggles** (with her cats) each knew Ulysses, and each mentions **Peter Dedalus**, the inventor who built the House of Mirrors and then vanished. Nestor, back home, admits he knew them all too (no more than that). *Canon: learning that Dedalus, Minaxo, Clio Biggles and Nestor are connected to Ulysses (MEDIUM). Clio Biggles stays offstage; her sister Cleopatra, who has the cats, speaks for the family (MEDIUM).*

### Part 3: the House of Mirrors

7. **Two houses in one.** Inside, mirrors and illusions. **Each twin sees a different house:** some walls are only on Jason's screen and some only on Julia's, and only some of each are solid. Neither view is the true one; they have to compare out loud to find the real way through. *Canon: illusions (HIGH). Split views are adaptation, the house's core asymmetric idea.*
8. **The owl clock.** A big clock shaped like an owl, high on a wall. **Jason** climbs to it and turns the hands; the time comes from **the guide** (Julia): "the House of Mirrors opens to visitors at a quarter past four". *Canon: owl clock (HIGH). Puzzle adaptation.*
9. **The Bakelite telephone.** When the clock strikes, the black telephone rings. A crackly recording in Dedalus's voice gives a riddle; the answer is a number Julia dials from the guide's old listings. *Canon: telephone (HIGH). Puzzle adaptation.*
10. **The chess game across the centuries.** A chessboard set mid-game, with Dedalus's note that it's "a game between centuries". **Julia** sees the next move in a mirror only she can see; the pieces are heavy brass, so **Jason** moves them. One right move opens Dedalus's study. *Canon: the chess game exists (HIGH). Position and solution are adaptation (ref: UNKNOWN).*
11. **Dedalus's study.** Notes and drawings: **there are other Doors in Kilmore Cove, and one key opens them all, the First Key** (canon reveal for book 3, MEDIUM). And, near the end, clues to where Dedalus went: **Venice** (canon: late in book 3, MEDIUM).

### Part 4: Oblivia

12. **The demolition crew.** Bulldozers outside: **Oblivia** has bought the House of Mirrors and means to knock it down (canon, MEDIUM). Manfred drives. A race: the twins must get out with Dedalus's notes before the walls come down (the mirror maze again, with pieces collapsing).
13. **The Lion Door.** From a hiding place, the twins watch Oblivia open a door behind the biggest mirror, the **Lion Door**, and step through to Venice (canon, MEDIUM–HIGH). The children **don't follow** (canon). Journal: Oblivia is going after Dedalus. *Book 4 hook.*

## Build order (proposal)

1. **Pass A:** breakfast, the shared journals, the expanded Kilmore Cove, the four impossibilities, the guide, the map marks, the old residents (steps 1–6).
2. **Pass B:** the House of Mirrors: split-view maze, owl clock, telephone, chess, the study (steps 7–11).
3. **Pass C:** Oblivia: demolition race and the Lion Door (steps 12–13).

## Status

- **Pass A done:** breakfast with Nestor starts book 3 and shares every journal entry so far; Jason gets his private memory of the map. `scripts/levels/kilmore_cove.gd` is now the whole village (the book 1 street and post office are unchanged): the square (statue of King William V, telephone box), Calypso's bookshop (atlases, A History of the Kings of England, the guide table), Miss Biggles's house and cats, the lighthouse and Minaxo, the railway to the boarded-up tunnel past the closed station, and the House of Mirrors (locked until Pass B). The checklist entry fills in as the four impossibilities are found; Calypso then gives Julia the guide; the marked landmarks are 3, 6 and 8 (any order); talking to Minaxo, Miss Biggles and Calypso afterwards gives the "Peter Dedalus" entry.

## Decisions

1. Split-view mirror house: yes.
2. Jason's memory of the map as his private clue: yes.
3. Chess: a short sequence of moves.
4. The demolition race stays.
